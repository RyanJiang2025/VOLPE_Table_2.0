# Implemented constraints and unresolved limitations

The reorganization preserves existing behavior and keeps policy decisions provisional.

## Implemented

- A separate five-story podium and thirty-story tower in the default parcel, with dedicated outdoor capacity and a shared ground-floor limit.
- Whole apartments and facilities, with continuous square-foot programs where declared.
- Housing selection minimums, aggregate use size limits, and configured office/lab exemptions.
- Bonus-authorized tower area subject to the independent physical tower cap.
- Optional required programs, development budget, land charged once, and the no-development alternative.
- Developer-funded public construction and ongoing upkeep, including negative NOI.
- Both annual surplus and valuation-based development profit, with independent result accounting and feasibility checks.
- Preference compensation frozen before joint optimization, complete housing blocks, and configurable nonhousing package rules.

## Unresolved

- Which financial objective should guide the project; cap rates and full development economics require calibration.
- Rental efficiency, vacancy, financing, lease-up, construction timing, transaction/selling costs, and ownership/transfer treatment of perpetual amenity liabilities.
- Floor-plan packing, common-area allocation, mechanical floors, and specialist use layouts.
- Multi-parcel interactions, existing floor area, transferable entitlements, and researched zoning/bonus rules.
- Preference threshold, premium size, normalization, portfolio benefit interactions, and alternative total bonus budgets.
- The legacy bonus unit uses tower footprint rather than the conventional parcel-area FAR denominator.

The prior checklist is preserved verbatim in [historical missing constraints](history/missing_constraints.md). Its older descriptions of implemented limits are historical and must not override active configuration or current decision records.
